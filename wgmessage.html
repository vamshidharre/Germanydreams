💡 Insider Tips for WG-Gesucht Success

⚡

Apply Within 15 Minutes

Turn on notifications on WG-Gesucht. Landlords receive 50+ messages in the first hour and usually stop reading after the top 20.

🇩🇪

Write in German

Even if your German is basic, sending your initial message in German increases your response rate by 3x compared to English.

🖼️

Upload a Friendly Photo

Profiles with a clear, smiling photo get 70% higher response rates than empty avatar accounts.

📜

Prepare Documents Early

Have your SCHUFA (or Blocked Account proof), enrolment letter, and ID ready in a single PDF to send immediately if asked.
