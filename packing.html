5. Luggage Distribution Guidelines

Airlines regulations & strict baggage allocation strategy for hassle-free transit.

🧳 Checked Baggage (23–40 kg)
  • Knives, scissors, and sharp cutlery (strictly packed here).
  • All packaged food items, spices, and heavy cookware.
  • Heavy winter clothing, shoes, and secondary toiletries.
  • Attach clear name tags and a distinct ribbon/strap on handles for easy identification.
🎒 Cabin & Laptop Bag (7–10 kg)
  • ALL original documents, passport, student visa, and cash.
  • Power Banks (strictly prohibited in checked luggage by aviation rules).
  • Laptops, chargers, external hard drives, and fragile electronics.
  • One extra pair of clothing and basic toiletries in case of transit/baggage delays.
