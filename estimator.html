💡 Essential Money-Saving Strategies

🛒

Shop at Aldi & Lidl

Discount supermarkets save 20–30% compared to REWE or EDEKA. Visit open-air markets near closing for cheap fresh produce.

🏏

Get the Deutschlandticket

€63/month for unlimited regional train and local transit across all of Germany. Exceptional value for students.

🍽

Eat at University Mensa

University canteens serve nutritious hot meals for €2–5. Eating at Mensa 3–4 times a week saves significant food money.

👥

Choose WG over Studio

A shared flat saves €200–400/month vs private studio apartments while providing an instant social circle in Germany.

👔

Werkstudent Jobs

Work up to 20 hrs/week during term. €12–16/hr is standard in IT, engineering, logistics, and university assistant roles.

🏭

Check BAföG & DAAD

International students may qualify for DAAD stipends or specific grants. Apply early before starting your degree.
